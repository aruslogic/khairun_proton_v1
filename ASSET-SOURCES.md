## S70 catalogue refresh — 4 October 2026

The current official combined S70 brochure lists 1.5 Lite, 1.5 Prime,
1.5TD Premium, 1.5TD Flagship and 1.5TD Flagship X. Executive is no longer
in that lineup and is removed from the current selector. Existing enquiries
with that old selection must choose a current variant.
